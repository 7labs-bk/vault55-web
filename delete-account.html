Delete your account on Vault 55

In the app

Open Profile → Delete account. Play stops at once. You can change your mind by signing in again before the deletion is carried out.

Without the app

Email 7labs7@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account on Vault 55”. We reply within 30 days. If you write from another address, we may ask you to confirm the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • Your identity details (legal name, country), your device details, your friends and your play progress.
  • Ingots and Coins you have not used. They cannot be restored.

What is kept

  • Records of rewards already sent, as accounting records, without your name.

A cash-out request in progress is decided first. A person carries out each deletion.
