Vault 55: Games to Earn Money

Vault 55: Games to Earn Money (“Vault 55”) is a mobile game for adults (18+). Play our own original casual games, clear levels and open the cases in the vault.

How it works

  • Coins are play currency for boosts and themes. They have no cash value.
  • Ingots are earned by clearing levels. 1,000 Ingots can be exchanged for a US$1 gift card once you reach the minimum.
  • Vault 55 never asks you for money, and never rewards ratings, reviews or installing other apps.
