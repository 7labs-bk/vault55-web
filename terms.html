Who can use Vault 55

You must be 18 or older. Players in every country can sign up. One account per person. The country you choose when you sign up cannot be changed later, and rewards are sent only to that country.

The Ingot rate

1,000 Ingots = US$1 in every country. How many Ingots a level pays depends on your country, because ads pay very different amounts in different countries. Your own rate is shown in the app, under “How rewards work”.

Milestone bonuses follow the same tiers: tier B pays two thirds and tier C one third of the tier A amount (10 Ingots at 10 levels cleared, 15 at 25, then 25 every 50), rounded down, never less than 1 Ingot. On top of the daily limit from levels, a player can get one milestone bonus a day.

Which country your rate is for. The country you choose when you sign up, unless your phone shows you are somewhere with a lower rate: then the rate is for that country. We check the country of your SIM card or, without one, of your internet address when you sign up. Your rate is set once. If it is wrong, for example because you moved, write to us and we will correct it for the future. Rewards are sent only to the country you chose.

Ingots are earned only by clearing levels and reaching level milestones: never from ads, from chance, from a purchase or from Coins.

Countries under sanctions

Because of sanctions laws, players in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia and Belarus can play every game and earn Coins, but cannot earn Ingots or cash out. The app says so when you choose your country.

The Master Vault

Clearing every case opens the Master Vault. From then on, the Ingots you earn from levels each day pay 10% more, rounded down, on top of the daily limit. The 1,000 Ingots = US$1 rate stays the same. The bonus can change only for the future, with notice in the app.

Not a bank

Vault 55 is a game. It is not a bank or a financial service: it holds no deposits, pays no interest, and never asks you for money.

Coins

Coins are play currency for boosts, streak freezes and themes. You get them from levels, stars, the daily streak, missions and optional rewarded ads. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, and never turn into Ingots.

Ads

The app shows ads at natural breaks, such as between levels, and never while you play. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one and get a fixed number of Coins, never Ingots, up to a daily limit. The Coins are added once the ad network confirms you watched the whole ad.

Rewards

Ingots can be exchanged for a reward at the rate above once you reach the minimum (5,000 Ingots, US$5) and meet the published rules. Amounts are small. Every request is reviewed by a person. Vault 55 never asks you for money.

What never earns anything

Rating or reviewing the app and installing other apps never earn Coins or Ingots. Inviting a friend earns Coins only, never Ingots: a Coin bonus shown on the Friends page, for you and your friend, once they have cleared enough levels on different days.

Fair play

Automated play, multiple accounts, or tampering with the app can lead to a request being held or rejected, or to the account being suspended. You are always told the reason and can appeal.

Changes

Rates, tiers and rules can change. A change is shown in the app before it applies, and it applies only from its date: Ingots already earned are never reduced.

Closing your account

You can delete your account at any time in Profile, or with the web form. Ingots that were not exchanged are lost when the account is deleted.
