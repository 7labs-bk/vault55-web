Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. Vault 55 is made by 7labs, Republic of Moldova (“we”). Questions: 7labs7@gmail.com.

What we keep

Your Google sign-in email, your country, that you confirmed being 18 or older, your play progress, and a record of every Coin and Ingot. To stop cheating: your phone model, system and app version, an install ID made by the app, your time zone offset, the country your SIM card, mobile network and internet address point to (only the country: we do not keep your IP address), and which ads were shown. Before a first reward: your legal name and payout email.

What we do not keep

Your year of birth is checked and then discarded. We do not ask for contacts, photos or precise location. The optional daily reminder is set on your phone only; we do not keep when or whether you get one.

Services we use

Google Firebase (Google LLC) signs you in with your Google account and runs our servers. Your account data is stored on Google Cloud. Countries are looked up from internet addresses with IP Geolocation by DB-IP.

Ads and ad networks

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies), which picks an ad from these networks: Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), Google AdMob (Google LLC) and Moloco (Moloco, Inc.). Any network added later is named here before it is used.

An ad network gets what it needs to show an ad: your phone's advertising ID, IP address, rough location from it, your phone model and system, and the ads you see or tap. Personalised ads are off unless you turn them on. When off, the networks may only use this to show, count and protect ads, not to build a profile of you. Their own policies: Unity, Google, Moloco.

Optional surveys

Vault 55 may offer an optional surveys section from BitLabs (Prodege, LLC) and CPX Research (CPX Research GmbH). It opens only after you agree on a separate screen, and you can stop at any time. These partners receive your Vault 55 player ID (never your name or email) and the answers you give them, which may include your age, gender, income and where you live, to match you with surveys. Their use of your answers is covered by their own privacy policies, shown before your first survey.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal data. With personalised ads on, ad networks may use it for ads targeted at you, which some US state laws call sharing. To opt out, turn off Personalised ads in Profile; it takes effect at once. You can also ask by email. Any report we give partners contains totals only, never data about one person.

Why we keep it

To run the games, to stop cheating and abuse, to show ads, and to send rewards.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. Fraud records and records of rewards already sent are kept as long as the law requires, without your name.

Your choices

You can see your balance and history in the app, change your ad choice in Profile, and delete your account at any time. To ask for a copy of your data or to correct it, write to us.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account erases your identity details. Records of rewards already sent are kept as accounting records, without your name.
